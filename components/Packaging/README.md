# Packaging

The front panels of the four packs, built from one component: `sw-pack` plus a variant class.

The variants are `sw-pack` (Original, forest and lime), `sw-pack--aloe` (lime and forest), `sw-pack--unscented` (fog and forest) and `sw-pack--singles` (spruce and lime). The parts are `__flap` (the peel-flap pill), `__mark` (SHAT), `__sub` (WIPES), `__line` (the tagline) and `__pills` (claims).

The fifth pack, the Clutch purse pouch, has its own card. The consumer provides the tagline and claims. The full rules are in the Packaging section of the brand book. These are screen mock-ups for direction; final dielines come from the packaging printer.
