# Shat Wipes

**No one has to know.** Personal wipes for people 18 to 40: gym bags, purses, festivals, commutes, first dates at someone else's place. The name is loud and the look is louder. Deep forest green with a jolt of lime, and huge heavy uppercase type that says the joke with a straight face. Everything else stays flat and clean. The product is about being discreet, but the brand doesn't whisper.

## Who it's for

People aged 18 to 40, all genders. They grew up with Dude Wipes on the shelf and meme-literate marketing, and they buy things they don't have to explain. They carry wipes in a gym bag, a purse, a glovebox or a desk drawer, and they want a pack that looks good coming out of any of them.

- **Gender-neutral on purpose.** No pink-for-her, blue-for-him packs, and no "for men" or "for women" line. The same forest and lime work for everyone; the Clutch pack exists because of where it goes, not who carries it.
- **Discreet by size, loud by design.** The joke is on the front of the pack, but every pack is small enough to disappear into a bag.
- **Speak like a friend, not a brand.** They can tell when marketing is trying too hard. Keep it dry.

## Voice

Snarky, confident and in on the joke. We say the thing everyone's thinking, then change the subject. We're cheeky, never gross: we wink at what the product is for, we don't describe it.

- **Short.** Headlines run 2–6 words and are set in the display face, uppercase.
- **Discretion is the running gag.** We keep acting like none of this is happening: "We don't have to talk about it."
- **Innuendo, not detail.** Say "back there", "your bottom line" or "a clean exit". Never describe bodily detail, and never show toilets.
- **Punch up.** Tease habits (dry paper, skipping the gym shower), never bodies, genders or groups.
- **Facts stay plain.** Counts, sizes and ingredients go in `micro` or `caption`, straight and unjoking.

## Visual foundations

**Colour.** `forest-ink` is the brand, both as a surface and as ink. `lime-voltage` is the one spark: primary buttons, active states and the SHAT wordmark on dark packs. Use one lime moment per section and never lime text on a light ground. Rhythm comes from inversion: `paper` sections, then a `linen-mist` band, then a full-bleed `forest-ink` section. Body text is `charcoal`, big headlines on light grounds are `obsidian` or `forest-ink`, and helper text is `slate`. `pebble` is for borders and placeholders only. `signal-blue` and `alarm-red` are rare, for a limited edition or a mock warning label. They never mean status.

**Type.** The display face is Inter Tight Black (900), the free stand-in for Wise Sans. It's always uppercase at 60px and up, with tight tracking and 0.85 leading (`display`, `heading-lg`, `heading`). Under 60px, switch to Inter 700 (`heading-sm`, `subheading`). Body copy is Inter 400 and buttons are Inter 500 (`label`). Heaviness is the signature, so don't soften it.

**Shape.** Buttons, tags, peel flaps and nav are full pills (`radius-pill`). Cards and inputs use `radius-card`, and big dark cards and pack corners use `radius-card-lg`. Nothing interactive has sharp corners.

**Elevation.** Flat. Cards get the `ring-subtle` hairline and inputs get `ring-inset`. Only floating things and pack renders use `lift-lg` (and `lift-xl` for the hero pack shot). No gradients and no blurs.

**Layout.** The content rail is `page-max` wide, and heroes and dark sections go full-bleed. Heroes are a centred stack of a huge headline, one line of `body-lg`, then a lime button with a text link beside it. Supporting sections use 2-column text and pack shot, or 3-column feature rows with a `space-32` gap.

## Imagery

Pack-first photography: packs on flat forest, lime or fog grounds, high-key light, a hard floor shadow. Show them in context (a gym bag, a festival bum-bag, a glovebox). Crops are square-cornered rectangles with `ring-subtle`, and round masks (`radius-pill`) are for avatars only. No bathrooms, no toilets, no toilet paper and no "in use" staging.

## Iconography

Line icons, 24px, 1.5px stroke, round caps. Use `forest-ink` on light grounds and `lime-voltage` on dark. There is no logo file yet: until one exists, the wordmark is **SHAT** set in `display` over a smaller **WIPES** in Inter 700 with wide tracking.
